# Privacy Policy - CruxCalculator

_Last updated: replace with the date you publish._

CruxCalculator ("the app") is designed so that your information never leaves your device.

## What the app does with data

* **Calculations, history tape, labels, notes, tags, variables, workspaces and settings** are stored only in private storage on your device.
* The app **does not collect, transmit, sell or share** any personal or usage data.
* The app has **no internet permission** and contains **no ads, analytics, crash-reporting, tracking or advertising SDKs**.
* There is **no account**, no sign-in and no cloud sync.
* Cloud backup and automatic device-to-device transfer of app data are switched off.

## Things you can choose to do

* **Copy / paste**: the app reads the clipboard only when you tap a paste action.
* **Export / share**: when you export your tape, the text is handed to the app or file location you choose. After that, the receiving app's privacy policy applies.
* **Vibration**: the app uses the vibration motor for key feedback. This needs the "vibrate" permission, which does not give access to any personal data.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data it stored. You can also clear it under Android Settings > Apps > CruxCalculator > Storage > Clear data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app update that introduces the change.
