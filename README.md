# CruxCalculator

A private, ad-free calculator for Android. Do everyday arithmetic, explore scientific calculations, convert units, and keep an editable calculation history—all designed to work offline.

## Why CruxCalculator?

- **Private by design:** no account, ads, or tracking. The app does not request internet access, and your calculations stay on your device.
- **Useful calculation history:** revisit, edit, label, search, pin, and organize previous calculations. If you change an earlier line, later results are updated or marked stale.
- **More than a basic calculator:** switch between everyday arithmetic, scientific functions, and unit conversions without leaving the app.
- **Make it yours:** choose from themes and adjust the accent color, key appearance, text size, and other display options.
- **Comfortable on different devices:** includes accessibility support and layouts for tablets and foldables.

## Everyday calculations

Use familiar operators and parentheses, with a live result preview. You can correct an entry, copy a result or expression, paste an expression, and optionally enable key vibration or sound.

Examples:

```text
(24 + 6) / 5
200 * 10%
```

## Scientific calculator

CruxCalculator includes trigonometric, logarithmic, power, root, factorial, and other scientific operations. Choose degrees, radians, or grads for angle calculations. Constants include π, *e*, and φ; combinations and permutations are also supported.

When possible, calculations retain exact fractions and repeating-decimal information instead of immediately rounding everything to a decimal.

## Unit conversions

Convert between units in 12 categories, with around 100 units available. You can enter a conversion directly in an expression:

```text
12 km to mi
5 kg to lb
72 F to C
```

Conversions work offline. Currency conversion is not included.

## Calculation tape and variables

Your calculation tape keeps a chronological record of your work. Tap a line to edit it, or add labels, notes, and tags to make entries easier to find. You can search, pin, duplicate, delete, and export calculations. Use `ans` to refer to the previous result.

You can also define a variable and use it in later calculations:

```text
rate = 0.18
200 * rate
```

## Helpful input and results

- Use implicit multiplication, such as `2(3 + 4)` or `2pi`.
- Get plain-language hints for common spelling mistakes in functions, variables, and units.
- Choose automatic, scientific, or engineering number notation in **Settings → Calculation → Number notation**. This changes how numbers are displayed, not the stored calculation.

## Privacy

CruxCalculator is built to work offline and does not request internet access. It does not require an account and does not include ads or tracking. Your calculation history and settings are stored locally on your device.

## Compatibility

CruxCalculator requires Android 8.0 (API 26) or later.

## Not included

This version does not include graphing, currency conversion, complex numbers, matrices, symbolic algebra, or programmer-mode calculations.

## Need help?

If something does not work as expected, open an issue in this repository and include the steps to reproduce it and your Android version. Please do not include private calculations or other personal information.
